# About Si_Sehat
Si_Sehat revolutionizes the way you manage your healthcare needs by offering a seamless and intuitive mobile experience. With just a few taps, users can book appointments with their preferred doctors, view available time slots, and even filter by specialization or location. 

The app ensures flexibility by allowing users to reschedule or cancel appointments hassle-free, making it perfect for today’s busy lifestyles. Si_Sehat also features appointment reminders to help users stay on top of their medical schedules, ensuring no critical appointments are missed.

Beyond scheduling, Si_Sehat provides access to essential medical records, allowing users to upload and store reports securely. This feature enables easy sharing with healthcare providers when needed. 

To enhance the experience further, Si_Sehat includes a built-in search tool to help users explore verified doctors, clinics, and hospitals, complete with reviews and ratings for informed decision-making. Whether you’re at home or on the go, Si_Sehat ensures your healthcare is just a tap away.
